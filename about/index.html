Studio3D

Jul 2025 – Dec 2025

A computer vision platform that turns photos and videos into 3D Gaussian Splatting models using 3D reconstruction and photogrammetry with COLMAP, owned end to end as the only engineer (97 commits). It has a Flutter app for capture and viewing, a Django REST API, Firebase authentication and Nginx in front.

Long-running GPU training jobs run on an asynchronous queue (Celery and Redis) with separate Docker workers for COLMAP, video processing, splat training and the web viewer. The public beta is live as a subscription with usage limits, project status tracking with ETAs and downloadable models.
