Work with me

I take on one consulting engagement at a time.

If you’re building something hard and need a senior engineering leader who can go deep (architecture, product decisions, and shipping), that’s the shape of the work. Clear scope. Direct collaboration. Depth over juggling many clients.

How it works

1

We align on the problem: what’s stuck, what “done” looks like, and whether I’m the right fit.

2

We run a focused engagement: hands-on building, advising, or both, for a defined stretch.

3

You leave with progress: shipped work, a clearer path, or both, not a slide deck that gathers dust.

A good fit

  • You have a real product or prototype, not only an idea slide.
  • You want a partner who will challenge assumptions and help you ship.
  • You’re okay with one senior person going deep rather than a large bench.

Probably not a fit

  • You need a large team staffed for months of undifferentiated feature work.
  • You’re looking for a vendor to “take requirements and disappear.”
  • You want a long list of parallel workstreams with no single owner.

Skills & domains

Useful when the engagement touches these areas (not a menu of separate offerings):

  • 3D Gaussian splatting, from research ideas to something people can use
  • Real-time and browser 3D, including splat rendering
  • Image and video generation products
  • End-to-end AI product work: architecture, shipping, and iteration
  • Python, Django, Go, TypeScript on AWS and GCP when that’s the stack in play

If that sounds useful, contact me and tell me what you’re building and where you’re stuck.
